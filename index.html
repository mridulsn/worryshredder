About worryshredder

Writing a worry down and then physically getting rid of it is an old trick: it gets the worry out of your head and onto something you can throw away.

Pick shred, burn or float away. The words aren't kept: the only thing remembered on this device is how many worries you've let go.

It's a small ritual, not therapy. If worries are taking over your days, a doctor or counsellor can really help.
